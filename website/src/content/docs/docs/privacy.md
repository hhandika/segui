## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SEGUL/SEGUI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles your personal information when you use our app or visit our website, [segul.app](https://segul.app).

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from our users. Our Service is designed to respect user privacy and the principle of data minimization.

## Crash Reporting

In the event of a crash or other technical issues within our Service, we may collect anonymous crash report data. This data does not contain any personal information and is used solely for the purpose of resolving technical issues and improving the Service.

## Website Analytics

Our website uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) to count page views anonymously. It does not use cookies, and it does not collect or store information that identifies you. It records aggregate statistics, such as the pages visited, the referring website, and your browser, operating system, device type, and country. We use these statistics only to understand which documentation is useful and to improve the website. The SEGUI app does not include this analytics.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [support@segul.app](mailto:support@segul.app).
